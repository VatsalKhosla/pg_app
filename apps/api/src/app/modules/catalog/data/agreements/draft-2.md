# {brand} owner agreement

**DRAFT for discussion. A lawyer hasn't reviewed it yet, and it isn't in force.**

This agreement is between you, the owner of the paying guest accommodation listed on {brand}, and [the company's registered name] ("we", "us"), which runs {brand}.

## 1. What we do

We list your PG, show it to students and their parents, take them on visits, and help them book a bed. A field agent visits your building to verify it before it goes live.

## 2. The placement fee

- You pay us a one-time placement fee for each tenant we place: 30% of one month's listed rent for the room category they book.
- The listed rent is the base rent on your listing. The fee is never worked out on a higher price set by a pricing rule.
- GST at 18% applies to the fee, as shown on our invoice.
- Students pay us no fee.

## 3. Who counts as a tenant we placed

A student counts as placed by us if we introduced them to your PG and they move in within 90 days of that introduction, however they pay. Our visit logs (the date and time, the field agent and where they were) record the introduction.

## 4. The token, and money owed

- A student secures a bed with a refundable token (₹2,000 by default), which counts toward their first month's rent.
- Nothing is due to us until the student has moved in and their 24-hour check window has passed without a dispute. If the room isn't as shown, the student's token is refunded the same day.
- If the student pays the rest of the first month's rent to you directly, the token counts toward our fee and we invoice you for the rest.
- We never pool money owed to you in our own account: anything paid out to you goes through our payment gateway's split settlement.

## 5. Your listing

- Your listing must be accurate: the rooms, the rents, the deposit and every charge a student pays.
- The rent quoted to a student is fixed for their whole stay.
- Your phone number and exact address are shown to students only once a visit is booked or a booking is confirmed.

## 6. Pausing or removing a listing

We may pause or remove a listing that breaks this agreement, including one where students are asked to pay outside {brand} to avoid our fee.

## 7. Changes

When we change this agreement, we give it a new version and ask you to accept it again before you use the owner area.
